How it works

One flight, six connected steps.

From scanning the field to a confident, actionable alert — nothing here needs a person walking rows.

01

NDVI Crop Health Mapping

Drone camera captures NIR + RGB imagery. Healthy plants reflect infrared light strongly — stressed ones don't. This becomes a colour heatmap of the whole field.

02

AI Pest & Disease Detection

A trained vision model scans the imagery for early signs of infestation or disease — often before symptoms are visible on the leaf.

03

Targeted Spraying

Flagged zones are exported as a spray map, so pesticide only reaches the patch that needs it — not the entire field.

04

Yield Prediction

Object detection counts plants, bolls, or heads directly from the flight footage, feeding a yield estimate weeks before harvest.

05

Drone + IoT Sensor Fusion

Aerial NDVI data is combined with ground IoT sensors — soil moisture, humidity, temperature — into one dashboard.

06

Prediction with Confidence Score

Every alert ships with a confidence value, so the farmer knows how sure the system is — and the model keeps improving with every flight.
