Why we exist

Most of what a field is trying to tell you never reaches the farmer.

Disease shows up on the leaf weeks after it starts inside the plant. Yield stays a guess until harvest day. And soil, humidity, and canopy data usually live in separate systems that never talk to each other. GreenSight exists to close all three gaps — not just one of them.

What we actually watch

Three signals, one picture.

Each on its own tells part of the story. Together, they tell you what's happening and why.

Disease & pest detection

Vision models trained on aerial imagery catch infestation and disease patterns before they're visible on the leaf — the earliest of the three signals.

Yield prediction

Plant, boll, and head counts from every flight feed a running yield estimate — so harvest size stops being a guess weeks before harvest day.

IoT ground sensing

Soil moisture, humidity, and temperature sensors sit in the field year-round, giving every aerial signal a ground-truth explanation.

Our approach

See it from above, confirm it from the ground.

Every field tells two stories — one from the air, in reflected light, and one from the soil, in moisture and temperature. GreenSight is built to read both at once, so a stress signal always comes with an explanation, not just a warning.

Business model

Data partnerships
